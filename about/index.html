About the artist

Polina Izvekova

Polina Izvekova is a realist painter and visual artist working primarily in oil painting and installation, with a background in illustration. Her practice combines a realist approach to image-making with site-specific and research-based projects grounded in fieldwork, archival research, and engagement with local communities.
